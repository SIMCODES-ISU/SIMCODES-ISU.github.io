## 📚 Research Project: Improving the Effective Fragmentation Potential (EFP) Method for Organic/Inorganic Systems

**Research Focus:** Evaluation of Effective Fragmentation Potentials for Solvated Metal Ion–Amino Acid Systems

**Description:** This project focuses on developing machine learning enhanced computational models for metal protein interactions, which play important roles in biological systems such as enzyme function and molecular recognition. Traditional quantum mechanical (QM) methods provide high accuracy but are computationally expensive for larger biomolecular systems. Using Effective Fragment Potential (EFP) methods, solvated metal ion and amino acid fragments were generated to create efficient models that maintain accuracy while reducing computational cost.
